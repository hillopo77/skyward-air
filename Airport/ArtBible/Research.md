Primary media: [FLEXUS official Steam listing](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4534960/Dear_Passengers/), its original ten screenshots and official trailer. `References/Sources.json` records direct original-media URLs and fingerprints. Promotional images are research-only and are not used as materials or advertised as project screenshots. The official terminal screenshot is id2, hash de1051493ef5690ab1a17d7c2fc35a74a42a4ea1. The other nine images provide the shared environment, material, lighting, props and airframe language. Public airport coverage is limited; the extended terminal layout and facilities here are original designs.

Observed in the terminal image: tall steel-framed windows with narrower inclined structural members; pale square tiles with subdued grout; a mezzanine with a substantial white fascia and dark handrail; long suspended white luminaires with warm endcaps; deep blue banners with a gold hem; contoured blue waiting seats and metal supports; muted metal posts with red queue belts; white/graphite scanner with an actual dark aperture and separate lead strips; metal-detector foot plates and top beacon; yellow information graphics on dark displays; vivid luggage, green plants and a bright aircraft view. The reference is airy and legible, rather than an entirely white empty hall. Its material response and structural detail remain more credible than its cartoon characters.

Implementation: original Skyward branding, a continuous departure route, separate arrivals reclaim, four security lanes, self-service and staffed check-in, physical bag props and conveyors, furnished gates, enclosed aircraft bridge, mezzanine lounge, cafe, retail, furnished restrooms, landside drop-off, ground-service equipment, ATC tower, hangar, taxiway, runway 18/36 and airfield lighting. Smooth manufactured profiles, real openings, shelf depth, supported hardware, varied prop sizes and contact are priorities. These additional facilities are authored extensions, not claims about areas shown by FLEXUS.

Rendering remains the established DX11/SM5 project path. Unreal captures, traversals and measured frame times determine delivery readiness. Blender authoring views are separately identified. The accepted aircraft and crash maps are preserved. This airport is a single-player editable prototype; no claim of a multiplayer backend, operational airport certification or new human characters is made.

Additional official trailer research: the published 30-frame contact grid (3-second intervals, References/Official_Trailer_ThumbnailGrid01.png) was inspected. It confirms the airport security area, steel/glass frontage, lush green context and yellow tactile warning paving outside the entrance. A modeled tactile strip was added at the forecourt crossing. This is a contact-grid review, not a claim that every trailer frame or the unreleased full game was inspected.
