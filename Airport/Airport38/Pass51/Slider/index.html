Walking in, before and after

The same camera spots photographed in the game before this pass and after it. The route now reads in one direction: in through the door, down the carpet to check-in, then left to security.

Drag across any picture (or focus it and use the arrow keys) to wipe between before and after.

Cloud Nine cafe, redesigned

Before: the old cafe, rows of identical tables on a bare floor. After: honey-oak floor, a lounge under the gallery beside a clear stair, a library wall in late sun, a window bar, a counter with menu boards, and golden-hour sun shafts through the west glass.

Gate lounges and the gallery

Each lounge redesigned around what the room is for and what it looks at. The waiting passengers were moved onto the new seating; the gate desks, boarding route and pickups stay where they were.

Security, the washrooms, and fixes

A 50 million brief for the screening hall, the WC passage and the washrooms, built around every lane, belt, scanner, door and fitting that works; plus the A08 sign flicker, the play corner and the gate agent.

In the plane

Before: the sky light leaked through the moving aircraft and lit every surface the same, from nowhere. After: no light on the ceiling at all; soft light from under the bins onto the seats, glowing path lights along the aisles, and luminous reading lights and call buttons.

Lighting, stripped and rebuilt

Real baked light: the ceiling fittings, the sun through the windows and the sky, bounced around the rooms by Lightmass.
