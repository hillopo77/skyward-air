# Cabin layout 06

The user said the broad result was good and asked for a believable separation between passenger seating and the cockpit, more room behind the pilots, and economy plus first/business seating with curtains. This change preserves the previous service equipment and reference palette. The complete preceding source and runtime reports are archived in `Versions/Interior_05_Before_Class_Split`.

From nose to tail, the current arrangement is: modern flightdeck; standing entry behind the pilots with observer jumpseat and crew equipment locker; reinforced cockpit door; galley/crew vestibule; forward passenger bulkhead with two aisle openings; two business rows; fitted class-divider bulkhead and gathered curtains; six economy rows; retained aft galley and two lavatories.

Both seating sections retain the requested two middle seats / 1–2–1 arrangement. Business has eight seats at 1.60m row pitch, navy upholstery, deeper/wider seats, supported calf rests, stowed arm tables and integrated rear displays. Economy has 24 seats at the existing 1.15m pitch. The partition has two gathered curtain pairs with real traverse rails, carrier eyelets, sewn hems and tiebacks. Continuous curved crown returns connect the partitions to the arched cabin liner. A business carpet inlay distinguishes the forward section.

The flightdeck extends 1.25m forward while its rear door stays at the vestibule. This creates approximately 1.20m of entry depth behind the pilot seats. The cabin receives two additional structural bays / 2.30m rather than squeezing the classes into the previous space. Window size and spacing remain unchanged. The aft service suite moves with the extended cabin. Exterior nose/tail geometry follows the extension; its separate production finish remains pending.
